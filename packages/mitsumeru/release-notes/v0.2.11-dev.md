# Mitsumeru 0.2.11-dev

Internal build — carries the `-dev` suffix; the update channel is `dev`.

Wraps DeepSeek Harness **0.2.2-rc.2** (from **0.2.2-rc.1**). rc.2 was published 2026-11-12T02:16Z
on DSH's `next` tag and promoted by the founder's call, not by the channel rule: at cut time
`latest` was still rc.1, and 0.2.10-dev (rc.1) was built and then superseded before it shipped.

## What changed

- **The pin.** 0.2.2-rc.1 → **0.2.2-rc.2**.

- **rc.2 ships its client bundles MINIFIED, and that broke both of our seams.** This is the first
  release where upstream changed how the code is emitted rather than what it says:

  | package | rc.1 | rc.2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | `dsh-client-ui-conversation` | 23,144 lines / 940 KB | **26 lines / 704 KB** |
  | `dsh-client-ui-plugin-manager` | 355 KB, pretty | 357 KB, 2 lines |

  Both patches were anchored to exact source lines, so both refused loudly at staging — which is
  what their guards are for. They are now **shape-driven** and work on either layout:

  - **Hero tagline** (`patch-hero-brand-tagline.mjs`): the factory's `children` table is found by a
    seat it contains (`conversation.hero.frame`) and brace-matched, no longer by `name:
    "conversation.content"` or `children: {` with a space; the new declaration is copied from the
    sibling seat's own value text, so `kind`/`scope` keep whatever quoting upstream used; the
    headline read is matched as `t("hero.headline")` **or** `t(\`hero.headline\`)`; and the inserted
    call is emitted compact for a minified bundle and spaced for a pretty one. The check script
    (`verify-hero-tagline.mjs`) slices `HeroShell` by matching its own braces instead of stopping at
    a `//#endregion` comment that no longer exists — it still renders the real function and still
    runs the slots service's negative control.
  - **Beta badge** (`patch-muen-beta-badge.mjs`): the anchor became
    `/beta:\s*EXPERIMENTAL_BUNDLES\.has\(pkg\.name\)/`, because the minified predicate is written
    `beta:EXPERIMENTAL_BUNDLES.has(pkg.name)` with no space after the colon. Edits are a match
    span, not a line.

- **The closure changed shape.** 340 `@deepseek-ai/*` packages (was 332), **42,568 files, 0
  symlinks** (was 39,058). Fourteen names are new — `dsh-agent-team`, `dsh-client-ui-agent-team`,
  `dsh-computer-use`, `dsh-cua`, `dsh-cua-bundle`, `dsh-experimental-cua-macos`, `dsh-mcp-manager`,
  `dsh-office-kit`, `dsh-terminal-panel`, `dsh-tool-agent-team`, `dsh-tool-office`, `dsh-error`,
  `dsh-util-ripgrep`, `dsh-client-ui-account-notifications` — and seven are gone, including
  **`dsh-deepseek-account-platform`** and `dsh-api-account-controller`. Upstream retired its account
  platform in this release.

- **The composition patch moved again.** `dsh-web-app/cordis.patch.yml` drops the `account-controller`
  row, adds `ui-account-notifications`, `mcp-manager`, `workspace-dependencies`, `skill-office` and
  `ui-agent-team`, and replaces `office-to-pdf` with `office-kit`. **Three new `!!js` gates** appear,
  all keyed to `ctx.get('profileContext')?.name === 'desktop'` — the `mitsu` profile is not
  `desktop`, so those rows stay off for us by construction.

- **The missing-edge pin still earns its keep** (`dsh-client-ui-slots` is still required by the
  renderer and still undeclared), and **`@muen/dsh-draw` still ships** exactly as 0.2.10-dev had it.

- **The supply-chain age gate did not object.** rc.2 was ~17 hours old at install — inside the 24 h
  cutoff — and `pnpm install` resolved it anyway (pnpm 11.7.0). Measured, not assumed: the exception
  list still names 0.2.0-rc.2 packages and was left untouched. If a future cut does trip the gate,
  the list is the place to extend, not the cutoff.

## What to check

- The app boots, the conversation renders, and **the hero still reads our headline** — the seam's
  whole purpose, and the piece that needed re-deriving.
- **Every `@muen/*` plugin still wears the Experimental badge** (Plugins page).
- **Settings → General → Account / avatar.** Upstream retired its account platform; our Muen sign-in
  is shell-side and does not depend on it, but that is the surface to look at.
- The sidebar dot (#FF5E00 / #FF0004), and the Draw tab.
- A profile that lists now-renamed `@deepseek-ai/dsh-experimental-agent-team*` bundles will log one
  skip line per stale name; that is the profile's business, not this build's.
